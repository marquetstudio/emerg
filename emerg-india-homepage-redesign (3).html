Our approach

Congratulations on discovering eMERG, a non-profit organization that empowers women entrepreneurs and professionals through training and sharing industry knowledge. You are embarking on an exciting journey, alongside like-minded individuals, who are dedicated to revolutionizing technology, business, and services.

eMERG, together with government and non-government departments, is here to support you and help you reach your full potential. With eMERG's resources and guidance, you can take a significant step towards becoming a leader in your field.

Let us come together to shape a world where women can achieve greatness and pave the way for others to follow. Welcome to eMERG — where every woman is equipped to thrive.

eMERG for you

Business Networking

With more than 300+ entrepreneurs, networking with eMERG is easy. You might find your next big customer as part of this group — and we open up further networking opportunities across our partner network.

Coaching & Mentoring

eMERG offers coaching, mentoring and skill development sessions to members. Drawing on real entrepreneurial experience, our senior members help newer members grow through hands-on mentoring and coaching.

Events & Seminars

eMERG's flagship event, FLLN (Forging Links and Leveraging Networks), anchors a calendar of seminars and gatherings for members — alongside international and domestic events run in collaboration with our partners.

Trade Delegations & Government Agency Support

eMERG's network spans government agencies, business groups, women's consortiums and external partners. We help members join trade delegations, investor meets and summits for business promotion.
